Privacy Policy

Bylka – receipt scanning app (hu.blokkolvaso.app) · Effective from 15 September 2026

This policy explains what data the Bylka app (the “App”) processes, why, for how long, and what rights you have. It is written in line with the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679).

2. What data do we process?

2.1. Using the app without signing in (guest mode)

Without signing in, your data is stored only on your own device (local storage of the browser/app). We do not upload your receipts to the cloud and we do not know who you are.

The one exception is AI recognition: the photographed receipt is forwarded through our server to the Google Gemini service so the text on the image can be converted into structured data (see section 3). The image is not retained by us after processing unless you are signed in.

To prevent abuse, the server counts requests tied to your IP address (daily/monthly allowance). This data alone does not identify you, and it is deleted once the allowance period expires.

2.3. Advertising

The App may contain ads. When ads are shown, Google AdMob processes your device’s advertising ID and the technical data required to serve the ad. Google’s own privacy policy governs this, and you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone settings at any time.

If you purchase the ad-free version, no ads are shown and no advertising data is processed.

2.4. Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card details; we only learn whether the purchase succeeded. Purchase records are kept by Google and by our entitlement service (RevenueCat).

4. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): the account, the sync and the purchase are needed for the App to work.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): moving from guest mode to an account, and processing the advertising ID when ads are shown.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): preventing abuse and exhaustion of the service (IP-based allowance), and debugging.

5. Retention

  • Account data and receipts: as long as your account exists. If you request deletion, they are deleted immediately and permanently.
  • Receipt photos: with the account, until the account is deleted.
  • IP-based AI allowance: until the daily/monthly period expires, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: under Google’s and RevenueCat’s own rules (which may be longer due to accounting obligations).

6. Your rights

You can exercise the following rights at any time via the contact address in section 1:

  • Access: request information about the data we process about you.
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate data (you can also edit receipts yourself in the App).
  • Erasure: request deletion of your account and all data. You can do this instantly in the App under Settings → Delete account, or by email on the account deletion page.
  • Portability: export your receipts in a machine-readable format (JSON) using the App’s export feature.
  • Objection and restriction: request restriction of processing or object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Complaint: you may lodge a complaint with the Hungarian supervisory authority (NAIH, naih.hu) or with the authority in your country of residence.

7. Cookies and local storage

The App does not use tracking cookies. The settings needed for operation (such as the selected language, theme and the guest allowance counter) are kept in your device’s local storage; they disappear when you leave the App or clear its data.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we become aware of such data, we delete it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Your account data can only be accessed with your own sign-in (protected by row-level security on the server). Receipt photos are kept in private storage: other users cannot list or open them, and displaying a photo uses a short-lived signed link that expires after about an hour.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we indicate the change in the App and on this page, updating the date above.
